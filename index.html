The studio

Why "Ninth Ember"?

When a fire dies down, the last embers still hold enough heat to start it again. We build the things that keep glowing: small, focused apps that people come back to, made by a team small enough to care about every detail.

  • Respect the player's time.No ads, no dark patterns, no manipulative timers. If something is fun, it does not need tricks.
  • Tune, then tune again.Crystal Road's balance was shaped by thousands of simulated playthroughs before real ones. We measure, then we adjust.
  • Ship small, ship often.A working build every week beats a perfect one next year.
  • Own what we make.Original music, original systems, and licensed art we credit by name.
